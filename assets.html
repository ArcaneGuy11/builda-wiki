Basic: Assets

1. Asset classification

In Builda engine, map assets are divided into six types (left image): Tiles, Deco, Actor, Prop, Items, and Weapons !

Note: Assets are not objects. Placing assets on the map creates objects. The icons for the two are also different in the engine.

Image: docs/images/asset1.jpg

There is only one type of UI asset (right image): UI.

Image: docs/images/asset2.jpg

2. Asset maps

Tiles

It is commonly used for overall ground paving and is placed at the lowest level of the display, such as for creating an overall atmosphere in landscapes, gardens, courtyards, the vast universe, and streets.

Deco

Based on the site, add some beautiful embellishments to the overall environment. For example, clouds on the horizon, distant mountains, and interior wall paintings.

Actor

The game features characters from different factions, such as the protagonist, NPCs, enemies, and monsters.

Prop

Functional materials are called devices, such as circular saws, fireballs, spikes, bombs, buttons, signs, monster doors, etc. In addition to the officially built-in devices, we can customize any device we want to use.

Item

Some collectible items, such as health potions, experience orbs, and gold coins.

Weapon

Anything that can cause harm when held in your hand, whether it's a melee weapon like a sword, spear, or halberd, or a ranged weapon like a pistol, submachine gun, staff, or anti-aircraft gun, is collectively called a weapon.

The assets themselves don't have any practical function; placing them on the map is purely for aesthetic purposes. They only unleash their full potential when different components are added.

The official built-in assets have already completed the "add different components" step for you, but when creating your own assets, you'll need to choose the appropriate components and assemble them yourself.

Referring to the official assets for learning is a great approach!

UI assets

In the game creation platform, the UI is divided into Map UI, controlUI, and objectUI .

The UI elements mainly include:

  • Buttons
  • Input boxes
  • Checkboxes
  • Sliders
  • Progress bars
  • Joysticks
  • Interactive buttons
  • Weapon/item joysticks
  • Skill joysticks
  • Jump buttons
  • Movement joysticks
  • Currency bars
